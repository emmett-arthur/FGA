Water temperature 68°F. Visibility improved to six feet on the western face — better than last year. Marine growth heavier on the Governors Island survey, particularly below the tidal zone. Noted for structural implications in the long-form report.

Warmer water and a calmer spring mean more growth, and more growth means more weight and more cover. Weight the structure can usually carry. Cover is the harder part — a heavy mat of growth hides the surface you are there to read, and it has to come off in patches before any honest assessment is possible.

## Conditions logged

- Water temperature: 68°F
- Visibility: ~6 ft, western face
- Growth: heavy below the tidal zone, Governors Island

None of it changes the structural picture yet. It does change the schedule — surveys here will take longer through late summer until the growth thins out again in the fall.
