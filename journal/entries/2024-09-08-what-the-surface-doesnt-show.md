---
title: What the surface doesn't show
date: 2024-09-08
location: New York Harbor
tag: Field Note
---

Underwater, a structure tells you things it won't from above. The spalling pattern, the marine growth lines, the way a pile moves under load — none of it reads the same from a dock. You need to be down there, close to it, to write a report that's worth anything.

A photograph from the pier deck gives you the top of the problem. It does not give you the waterline, where most of the deterioration actually lives, and it never gives you the mudline, where the pile either still has footing or doesn't. Those are the two places that decide whether a structure has ten more years or two.

So the work is slow on purpose. Hand on the structure, light close to the surface, reading it the way you'd read anything you mean to be honest about — up close, in person, in its own conditions.
