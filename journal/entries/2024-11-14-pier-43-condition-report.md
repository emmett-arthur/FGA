Visibility around four feet. Eastern face shows concrete spalling at the waterline on two piles — consistent with what was noted last season. The rest held. Full condition report and photographic record filed. Recommend follow-up on piles 7 and 11 before spring.

The spalling pattern follows the tidal line almost exactly. Where the concrete sits wet-then-dry through the day, the freeze-thaw cycle has started to open the surface. It is early, but it is the kind of thing that turns into a real repair if it is left for two or three winters.

## What was surveyed

- All 14 piles on the eastern face, waterline to mudline
- Fender system and dock hardware above the splash zone
- Marine growth sampling at three depths

Piles 7 and 11 are flagged for follow-up. Everything else is within tolerance for the structure's age and use. The long-form report, with the full photographic record, has been filed with the owner.
